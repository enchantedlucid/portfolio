Who I Am

Background

I'm Nurul Fatnin Haidah — an Interactive Media developer with a TVET-trained, technically practical background in software development, web application development, database systems, and cybersecurity. I'm based in Setiawangsa, Kuala Lumpur, currently in Year 3 of my Computer Science (Interactive Media) degree at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM).

I solo-built a full C++ attendance and leave management system with role-based access control and MySQL, and have gone on to build a Java/Swing ordering system, a PHP/MySQL QR restaurant website, and a Python AI waste classifier. My Final Year Project studies VR locomotion and motion sickness using Unity XR and the Simulator Sickness Questionnaire (SSQ) methodology.

I work at the intersection of engineering and design — equally comfortable writing RBAC logic in C++ as I am directing a short film or designing a motion graphics reel — and use AI tools as part of my workflow to prototype faster.

Outside of coursework, I serve as Vice President of the Motion Graphics Final Exhibition and have held part-time and volunteer roles ranging from accounting to concert crew.
