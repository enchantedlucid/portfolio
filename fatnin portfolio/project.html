Short Film · Workshop II · Director & Video Editor
PARALLEL — Short Film

Two versions of the same person living in parallel worlds — one struggling with unemployment, the other living a successful but empty life. When they discover a mysterious mirror connecting their realities, they make a fateful decision to switch lives. A sci-fi psychological drama produced over 8 weeks with a crew of 4, screened at the faculty showcase on January 15, 2026 at Lab Multimedia 3, FTMK UTeM.
